Nicky J

Photographer  ·  Storyteller  ·  Wanderer

I'm drawn to the in-between — liminal spaces where city neon bleeds into overcast sky, where sunlight pushes through a canopy of trees on the edge of somewhere you can't quite name.

My work lives where nature meets nightlife. The sea under grey clouds. A palm silhouetted against a skyline flickering to life. Dark, dramatic light that turns an ordinary street into something cinematic.

Photography, film, graphic design, websites — whatever the medium, I'm chasing the same thing: that strange, beautiful quality of light that makes you stop and feel it.
