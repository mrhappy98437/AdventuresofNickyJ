Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

MailBase is a locally running desktop email client. It accesses email data only after the user authorizes an email account.

Google user data

When Gmail is connected, MailBase uses Google OAuth and the Gmail API to provide email-client functionality, including reading and synchronizing messages and metadata, displaying message content and attachments, composing and sending messages, replying and forwarding, and performing user-requested message-management actions.

Use and storage

Google user data is used only to provide MailBase functionality to the authorizing user and is not used for advertising. MailBase may store synchronized email information and application data locally on the user's computer.

Sharing

MailBase does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties except when necessary to perform an action explicitly requested by the user or as required by law.

Authorization and deletion

Users control which Google accounts they authorize and can revoke access through their Google Account security settings. Users can also remove locally stored MailBase application data from their computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated if MailBase's functionality or data practices change.
