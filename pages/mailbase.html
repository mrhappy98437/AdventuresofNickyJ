MailBase

A private, locally running desktop email application.

MailBase brings your email accounts together in a clean, database-style desktop app for reading, organizing, searching, composing, sending, replying to, forwarding, and managing email.

▣

Private by design

MailBase runs locally on your computer. Synchronized application data is stored locally rather than on this website.

✉

Works with Gmail

Gmail accounts connect through secure Google OAuth and the Gmail API after the account owner authorizes access.

⚙

Simple and focused

A clean interface built around the email tools that matter, without turning this website into the email application itself.
